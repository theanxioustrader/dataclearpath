Questions about prices.

How much does a spreadsheet cleanup cost?

Tidy-up is $299 per project, planned for about 1 week. The $49 Spreadsheet Checkup gives you written recommendations within 3 days of your upload. We confirm exactly what's included before we start.

What if I'm not satisfied with my Tidy-up?

Reply to your delivery email within 30 days of delivery and tell us. We'll refund you in full to the way you paid. We make refunds by hand, and it can take a few days to show on your statement. This applies to the Tidy-up only. Your files are deleted 30 days after delivery, as described in our privacy notice.

Does the $49 come off a package?

Yes. The $49 package credit comes off any package you buy from us. For example, Tidy-up becomes $250.

What is not included?

Anything over the limits for tables, screens, forms and users is quoted before we start. You pay your own tool and hosting fees directly.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You own everything we build.
