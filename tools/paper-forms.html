Our accuracy promise

We flag anything we're unsure of. If more than 2% of the cells we didn't flag are wrong, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll fix them free, or refund you if you prefer.

What not to upload

Please don't upload medical records, ID documents, payroll or Social Security numbers, or bank card numbers. If we find any, we stop, delete the file and refund you.
