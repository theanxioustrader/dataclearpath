Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

DataClearPath helps small businesses move from paper and messy spreadsheets to clean digital systems. This notice explains, in plain English, what we collect when you use this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, type of business and your message. The form is delivered to us by Formspree, our form service.
  • Spreadsheet Checkup: your email, optional notes and one spreadsheet file.
  • Payments: you pay $49 by PayPal. PayPal handles the payment; we receive a payment record (order ID, amount, date) and your email. We never see or store your card details.
  • Email and phone: if you email or call us, we keep that conversation so we can reply.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you, to do the work you asked for (for example, write your Checkup report), and to send you a quote if you ask for one. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How your Checkup file is handled

  • To draft your report, we send Anthropic's AI a summary of your spreadsheet (its structure, the problems we found) and a few sample rows, together with your notes. The full file stays on Cloudflare. A person reviews every report before it's sent.
  • Your file and the report are deleted 30 days after we send the report. We keep your file name and notes for up to 12 months after that, and your email address with the payment record for up to 7 years for tax and refunds. Ask us to delete your personal details sooner and we will do it within 30 days, except the payment records we are required to keep.
  • If you start a checkup but don't complete payment, your file is removed in our monthly cleanup.

Who else handles your information

Only the services we need to run this site and do the work: Formspree (contact form), PayPal (payments), Anthropic (the AI tools that help draft Checkup reports), Resend (emails about your order), our email provider (replies), Cloudflare (stores Checkup files and reports, and delivers this website) and GitHub (hosts this website). Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs, so your browser connects to those services when you visit.

Your choices

  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@dataclearpath.com. We'll do it within 30 days, except the payment records we are required to keep.
  • If we email you and you'd rather we didn't, reply "stop" or email us, and we won't contact you again.
